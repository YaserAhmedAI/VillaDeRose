Our Story

A Family Home, Open to the World

Villa De Rose began as a decade-old family home, lovingly restored to welcome guests from every corner of the globe. Every room tells a story — from the hand-carved wooden panelling in the rooms to the rose garden that inspired our name. We believe that true hospitality lives in the details: the smell of fresh linen, homemade jam at breakfast, and a host who remembers your name.
